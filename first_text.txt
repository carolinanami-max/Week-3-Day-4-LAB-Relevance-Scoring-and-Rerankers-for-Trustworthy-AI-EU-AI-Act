Question: What is AI literacy?

Answer: AI literacy refers to the understanding of artificial intelligence capabilities and limitations, as well as the 
ability to effectively integrate AI tools into existing workflows. It encompasses training that equips participants with 
foundational knowledge of AI, emphasizes security best practices, and promotes transparency and human oversight in AI usage. 
Key components of AI literacy include establishing an AI governance model, defining roles and responsibilities, and appointing 
trained advisors to foster AI knowledge within an organization.
